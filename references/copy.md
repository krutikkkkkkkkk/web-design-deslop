# Deslopping web copy

Generated copy has a recognizable texture: abstract nouns, stacked adjectives, symmetrical structures, and claims with no referent. It sounds confident and says nothing. The fix is always the same move: replace the abstraction with the specific thing.

## The tells

### Vocabulary
These words almost never survive a rewrite: unlock, elevate, empower, supercharge, streamline, seamless, seamlessly, effortless, effortlessly, transform, revolutionize, reimagine(d), next-gen, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, all-in-one, powerful, robust, intuitive, innovative, game-changing, world-class, best-in-class, holistic, synergy, leverage (verb), harness, unleash, journey, ecosystem, solutions, experience (as a noun for a product), delight, crafted with care, built for modern teams, designed for humans.

Not banned; just notice that each one is standing where a fact should be. "Seamless checkout" → "Checkout in one screen, UPI or card." "Powerful analytics" → "See which SKU sold out and when."

### Structures
- **Triplets everywhere.** "Fast. Simple. Secure." / three adjectives in every subhead / three bullets under every heading. Rhythm substituting for content. Swap: one thing, or the actual number of things.
- **"Not just X. Y."** / **"X, reimagined."** / **"Meet [Product]."** / **"Say goodbye to…"** / **"Whether you're a … or a …"** / **"It's that simple."** / **"The future of…"** / **"Built for…"** / **"…made easy"**. Template sentences. Swap: say the thing plainly.
- **Rhetorical questions as headlines.** "Tired of messy spreadsheets?" Swap: the statement.
- **Adjective + noun feature titles.** "Lightning-fast performance", "Bank-grade security", "Real-time collaboration". Swap: what it does, or what the user gets. "Loads in under a second on 3G." "Two people can edit the same invoice."
- **Closing with "Ready to get started?" / "Join thousands of…" / "Start your free trial today".** Swap: a closing line specific to the brand and one named action.
- **Em dashes and colons doing the work of a sentence.** "Fast, reliable, secure — everything you need." Swap: a sentence with a verb.
- **Parallel symmetry.** Every feature blurb the same length, every heading the same shape. Real writing is uneven because real things are.
- **Emoji in headings or bullets.** Never.
- **Title Case On Every Heading.** Pick sentence case unless the brand has a reason.

### Claims
- Round, unsourced numbers: "10,000+ users", "99.9% uptime", "Trusted by 500+ companies". Swap: the real figure with its specificity, or no figure. `{{TODO: real number}}` if unknown.
- Fake testimonials and fake names. Swap: a real one or none.
- "No credit card required" / "Cancel anytime" as the second line under every CTA. Fine once, if true; a tell as reflex.

## The rewrite method

1. **Find the referent.** For each sentence, ask: what actual thing, feature, number, place or person is this about? If there is none, the sentence is removed or replaced with one that has one.
2. **Use the customer's words.** How would a buyer describe the problem to a friend? That vocabulary beats marketing vocabulary. A dealership customer says "I want to see the car and know the on-road price", not "a seamless vehicle discovery experience".
3. **Use the brand's voice.** A specialty roaster, a preschool and a law firm do not write the same way. Decide the register (warm, dry, blunt, technical, playful) from the brand material and hold it.
4. **Say what happens next.** CTAs name the action and its result: "See this week's roasts", "Book a visit", "Send a test invoice". Not "Get started", "Learn more", "Explore".
5. **Cut by half.** Generated copy is padded. Remove the sentence that restates the previous one. Headlines: under ten words, usually under six. Subheads: one sentence with a verb and a noun the reader recognizes.
6. **Let it be uneven.** One feature gets a paragraph because it matters; another gets four words. One section has no heading. This asymmetry is what reads as written by a person.

## Examples

**Hero, before:**
> Unlock seamless invoicing for modern teams
> Infinity Invoices empowers freelancers and agencies to create, send and track beautiful invoices effortlessly — all in one place.
> [Get started free →] [Learn more]

**Hero, after:**
> Send the invoice. Get paid. Know which ones are late.
> Invoicing for freelancers and small studios in India. GST-ready, UPI links on every invoice, reminders that go out without you.
> [Send a test invoice]

**Feature card, before:**
> ⚡ Lightning fast
> Experience blazing performance with our optimized infrastructure.

**Feature row, after:**
> Loads in under a second
> The whole app is 90 KB. It works on a 3G connection in a Vadodara basement, which is where we tested it.

**Closing, before:**
> Ready to transform your workflow? Join thousands of happy users today.

**Closing, after:**
> If you send more than five invoices a month, this will save you an afternoon. Try it on your next one.

**Preschool, before:**
> Nurturing young minds for a brighter tomorrow
> A holistic learning environment where every child can thrive.

**Preschool, after:**
> Two teachers for every twelve children, and a garden they are allowed to dig in.
> Lotus Juniors, Vadodara. Ages 2 to 6. Visit on a weekday morning and see a class.
